See it on, before it arrives.

Turns on your camera and tracks your face live in the browser — nothing is recorded or uploaded.

What this is: live face tracking in the browser, with six frames from the Leidmann collection (Matsuda, Jacques Marie Mage, Cartier, Kuboraum and Akoni) built from leidmann.com product photography and placed in 3D on your head — the front sits on the bridge of your nose at its real width, taken from each frame’s lens, bridge and temple measurements, and the temples run back to your ears, so turn your head to see them from the side. Size is estimated from average eye spacing, so it can be off by a few percent on very narrow or wide faces. A production version would use the same pipeline across the whole catalogue.
Prototype build v7 · runs entirely in this browser · no data leaves your device
